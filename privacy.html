Privacy Policy

Clinic Appointment Assistant · Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains how personal information is collected and used by the appointment scheduling service operated by Marcos Lacayo in Austin, Texas (the “Service”). The Service answers phone calls and messages for a medical clinic, schedules appointments, and sends appointment confirmations.

Mobile information and text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service or message delivery, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade mobile phone numbers or SMS consent to anyone, under any circumstances. Phone numbers collected for appointment messaging are used solely to send that patient messages about their own appointment.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to schedule an appointment and confirm it:

  • Mobile phone number — provided when you call or message to schedule.
  • Name — as you provide it, to hold the appointment.
  • Appointment details — service requested, location, date and time.
  • Registration form details, if you choose to complete one: date of birth, address, preferred language, and an emergency contact.

The Service does not ask for, record, or store symptoms, diagnoses, medications, medical history, or any other clinical information. It schedules appointments only.

How we use your information

Your information is used only to:

  • Schedule, reschedule, or cancel your appointment
  • Send you a written confirmation of that appointment
  • Send you a link to the patient registration form
  • Send you a reminder before your appointment

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to your appointment.

How you consent

Consent is collected verbally during a phone call, or in writing during a WhatsApp conversation, at the time you request an appointment. Before your number is stored you are told that message and data rates may apply, that message frequency varies, and that you can reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Your number is stored only after you agree. Consent is never pre-checked, never bundled with anything else, and never required in order to receive care.

Service providers

We use the following subcontractors strictly to deliver the Service. They process information only on our instructions and may not use it for their own marketing:

  • Twilio — SMS delivery and telephone service
  • Meta Platforms — WhatsApp message delivery
  • Google — appointment calendar storage
  • Anthropic — processing conversation text so the assistant can respond

As stated above, mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with these or any other parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

How long we keep it

Appointment records are retained in the scheduling calendar for as long as needed to provide care and meet recordkeeping obligations. Conversation history is held temporarily and discarded within a few hours. You may request deletion at any time.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts immediately.
  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Decline the registration form — it is optional.

To make a request, email us at the address below.

Security

Access to appointment information is limited to the operator of the Service and the clinic staff who need it to provide care. Information is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Children

The Service is not directed at children. Appointments for minors are scheduled by a parent or guardian, who provides the contact information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new revision date.
