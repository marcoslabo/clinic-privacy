Messaging Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 23, 2026

These terms apply to the SMS and WhatsApp appointment messaging program operated by Marcos Lacayo (the “Service”), an appointment-scheduling assistant that answers calls and messages on behalf of a medical clinic.

Development notice. This Service is currently a working prototype operated by an individual developer for testing and demonstration. It is not yet deployed for general public use.

1. Program description

By providing your mobile number when you schedule an appointment, you agree to receive text messages about your own appointment. These are transactional messages only:

  • Confirmation of an appointment you requested
  • Notice that an appointment was rescheduled or cancelled
  • A link to the patient registration form
  • Reminders before a scheduled appointment

We do not send marketing or promotional messages through this program.

2. How you opt in

You opt in verbally or in writing when you contact the clinic to schedule an appointment, by phone or by WhatsApp, and provide your mobile number for the purpose of receiving a confirmation. We do not add numbers obtained any other way, and we do not purchase or import phone number lists.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your own appointment activity. A typical appointment generates one confirmation message, plus a reminder, plus additional messages only if you reschedule or cancel.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge you for these messages, but your mobile carrier may, depending on your plan. Contact your carrier for details about your plan.

5. How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message at any time to be unsubscribed. You will receive a single confirmation that you have opted out, and no further messages after that. You may also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT.

Opting out of text messages does not affect your ability to schedule an appointment by phone or to receive care. To rejoin, reply START, or simply provide your number again when you next schedule.

6. How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at marcos.bosche@nymbl.app.

7. Supported carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier and is not guaranteed.

8. What we will never send

This program never sends medical information, test results, diagnoses, or clinical advice by text message. Messages contain scheduling details only: date, time, location, and service booked. The Service does not collect or store symptoms, diagnoses, medications, or medical history.

9. Privacy

Your phone number is used only to send you the messages described above. We do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what is collected, who it is shared with, and how long it is kept.
