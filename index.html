Clinic Appointment Assistant

Bilingual (Spanish and English) appointment scheduling for a medical clinic in Austin, Texas.

What this service does

This service answers incoming phone calls and WhatsApp messages for a medical clinic in Austin, Texas. It answers questions about clinic hours, locations, services and prices, and schedules appointments for patients who request one. It operates in Spanish and English.

After a patient schedules an appointment, the service sends that patient a written confirmation by SMS or WhatsApp containing the date, time, and address of their visit.

Messages we send

All messages are transactional and relate only to the recipient's own appointment:

  • Confirmation of an appointment the patient requested
  • Notice that an appointment was rescheduled or cancelled
  • A link to the patient registration form
  • A reminder before a scheduled appointment

We do not send marketing or promotional messages. Message frequency varies based on the patient's own appointment activity. Message and data rates may apply.

How patients consent to receive messages

Consent is collected verbally during a phone call, or in writing during a WhatsApp conversation, at the moment the patient asks to schedule an appointment. There is no web form and no pre-checked box; the patient must actively agree.

Before any number is stored, the assistant reads this disclosure aloud or sends it:

English: “I can send your appointment confirmation by text message to this number. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Our privacy policy and terms are on our website. Is that okay?”
Español: “Te puedo mandar la confirmación de tu cita por mensaje de texto a este número. Pueden aplicar tarifas de mensajes y datos. La frecuencia de mensajes varía. Responde STOP para cancelar, HELP para ayuda. Nuestra política de privacidad y términos están en nuestro sitio web. ¿Está bien?”

The patient's number is stored only after they answer yes. Declining does not affect their ability to schedule an appointment or receive care. We never purchase, rent, import, or otherwise acquire phone numbers from any third party.

Documented opt-in process

The complete opt-in process — including the live web opt-in form with its unchecked consent checkbox, and the exact verbal disclosure script in English and Spanish — is documented at SMS Opt-In Process.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately. Reply HELP for assistance. Full details are in our Messaging Terms & Conditions.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
