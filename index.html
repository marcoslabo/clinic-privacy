Privacy Policy — Appointment Messaging

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains how phone numbers and personal information are handled by the appointment-scheduling assistant operated by Marcos Lacayo (the “Service”). The Service answers phone calls and messages on behalf of a medical clinic, answers questions about services and hours, and schedules appointments.

Development notice. This Service is currently a working prototype operated by an individual developer for testing and demonstration. It is not yet deployed for use by the general public, and no production patient data is processed through it.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to schedule an appointment and confirm it:

  • Mobile phone number — captured automatically when you call or message the Service.
  • Name — as you provide it, to hold the appointment.
  • Appointment details — the service requested, location, date and time.
  • Registration form details, if you choose to complete one: date of birth, address, preferred language, and an emergency contact.

The Service does not ask for, record, or store symptoms, diagnoses, medications, medical history, or any other clinical information. It schedules appointments only.

How we use text messages

If you provide your mobile number when scheduling, you may receive SMS or WhatsApp messages related to your own appointment:

  • Confirmation of an appointment you requested
  • Notice that an appointment was rescheduled or cancelled
  • A link to the patient registration form
  • Reminders before a scheduled appointment

These are transactional messages only. We do not send marketing or promotional messages, and we never sell, rent, or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies and depends on your appointment activity.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message to opt out immediately. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the details below. Opting out of text messages does not affect your ability to schedule an appointment by phone.

Who we share information with

Information is shared only with the service providers needed to operate the Service:

  • Twilio — to deliver SMS messages and handle phone calls
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) — to deliver WhatsApp messages
  • Google Calendar — to store appointment times
  • Anthropic — to process conversation text so the assistant can reply

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

Appointment records are retained in the scheduling calendar. Conversation history is held temporarily in memory to maintain the thread of a conversation and is discarded within a few hours. Registration form submissions are retained only for the duration of the testing period and are deleted when testing concludes.

Your choices

  • Opt out of messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Request a copy of the information held about you.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Decline to complete the registration form — it is optional.

To make any of these requests, use the contact details below.

Children

The Service is not directed at children. Appointments for minors are scheduled by a parent or guardian, who provides the contact information.
