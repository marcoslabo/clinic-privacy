SMS Opt-In Process

Clinic Appointment Assistant · operated by Marcos Bosche · Austin, Texas
Documentation of how end users give consent to receive text messages.

This page documents every way an end user can consent to receive SMS messages from the Clinic Appointment Assistant, operated by Marcos Bosche. There are two opt-in paths, both shown below. Both require active, affirmative consent. Nothing is pre-checked, and consent is never bundled with anything else.

Opt-in path 1 — Web form

Patients can request appointment text messages using the form below. The consent checkbox is unchecked by default and the form cannot be submitted without checking it.

Request appointment text messages

The consent box above is unchecked by default. Active consent is required.

Thank you. You are now subscribed to appointment text messages. Reply STOP to cancel.

Opt-in path 2 — Verbal, during a phone call or WhatsApp conversation

Most patients schedule by phone or WhatsApp. When they do, the scheduling assistant asks for consent out loud before storing the number. The exact wording is scripted and identical every time. This is the disclosure end users hear:

English script

Clinic Appointment Assistant · (512) 883-4184
I'd like to book an appointment for tomorrow morning.
I can send your appointment confirmation by text message to this number. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Our privacy policy and terms are on our website. Is that okay?
Yes, that's fine.
Confirmed. You'll get a text with the date, time and address.

Spanish script

Clinic Appointment Assistant · (512) 883-4184
Quiero agendar una cita para mañana en la mañana.
Te puedo mandar la confirmación de tu cita por mensaje de texto a este número. Pueden aplicar tarifas de mensajes y datos. La frecuencia de mensajes varía. Responde STOP para cancelar, HELP para ayuda. Nuestra política de privacidad y términos están en nuestro sitio web. ¿Está bien?
Sí, está bien.
Listo. Te llega un mensaje con el día, la hora y la dirección.
The number is stored only after the patient answers yes. If the patient declines, no messages are sent and the appointment is scheduled normally. Consent is never a condition of receiving care.

What subscribers receive

  • Confirmation of an appointment they requested
  • Notice if that appointment is rescheduled or cancelled
  • A link to the patient registration form
  • A reminder before the visit

All messages are transactional and relate only to the recipient's own appointment. No marketing or promotional messages are sent. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Example message

From (512) 883-4184
Cita confirmada - Clinica Hispana Rubymed Austin. Consulta medica general. Jueves 24 de septiembre a las 3:00 PM. Sede Riverside, 2410 E Riverside Dr Suite G-2, Austin TX. Costo: $25. Para cambiarla o cancelarla llame al (512) 271-9357. Responda STOP para no recibir mensajes.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately. Reply HELP for assistance. Opting out does not affect the patient's ability to schedule an appointment or receive care.

Sourcing of phone numbers

Every number is given directly to us by its owner, for this purpose, through one of the two paths above. We do not purchase, rent, lease, import, or otherwise acquire phone numbers from any third party, and we never message a number that was not given to us directly.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
